## 3. Scene-by-Scene Storyboard Prompts (For Multi-Shot Generation)

### Shot 1: Water Inflow & Infiltration (0s – 4s)
- **Camera Motion:** Slow forward dolly with shallow depth of field.
- **Prompt:**
  > `Cinematic macro shot of clear water flowing into an industrial water treatment filtration manifold, pristine brushed steel pipes, high-precision brass and chrome pressure meters with glowing backlit digital displays, studio rim lighting, Arri Alexa 65, 8k, photorealistic fluid caustics.`

### Shot 2: Dual-Tank Softener System & Valve Technology (4s – 8s)
- **Camera Motion:** Smooth orbital arc shot from left to right.
- **Prompt:**
  > `Aesthetic commercial hero showcase of a luxury residential and commercial dual-tank water softener system, cylindrical matte white and deep slate composite tanks, digital control head with subtle blue status indicator light, connected copper and PEX plumbing, high-tech sanitary engineering, soft bounce lighting.`

### Shot 3: Purity & Softened Water Transformation (8s – 12s)
- **Camera Motion:** High-speed 120fps slow-motion macro tracking.
- **Prompt:**
  > `Ultra slow motion 120fps macro shot of silky, perfectly softened water pouring smoothly from a luxury modern gooseneck faucet into a pure clear glass vessel, crystal reflections, micro water droplets suspended in mid-air, pristine clean kitchen and wellness aesthetic, vibrant lighting, zero hard-water mineral spots.`

### Shot 4: Industrial Plant & Architectural Scale (12s – 18s)
- **Camera Motion:** Slow pull-back aerial perspective with smooth deceleration.
- **Prompt:**
  > `Wide architectural cinematic pan of a large-scale commercial water softening and filtration facility, array of vertical composite ion-exchange vessels, polished stainless steel header pipes, clean white sterile facility, soft diffuse overhead daylighting, premium industrial automation aesthetic.`
